Arduino and Espressif are two brands that manufacture General Purpose MicroControllers.
Raspberry PI is also one such...

|Topic|Arduino|ESP32|
|------|--------|------|
|Architecture|8-bit|32-bit|
|Clock|48 MHz|240MHz|
|I/O Pins|20 Digital, 6 Analog In, 1 Analog out|38 to 77 I/O Pins|
|Connectivity|Wired|Wireless (Bluetooth and Wifi builtin)|
|Memory|256KB Flash|4MB Flash|
