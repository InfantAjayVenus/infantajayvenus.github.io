### Focus on Learning
1. Don't rush to contribute or show  case your knowledge, BE PATIENT.
2. Ask questions, take notes, DON'T ASSUME!

### Build Relationships
1. Meet with every one, know their work
2. Get as much info as possible. Get all the assets you can from them
3. Check if you can help them with something...
4. Check who they collaborate with and it's details, why?, to What extent?

### Set Clear Goals
1. Connect with your manager/team to set a very specific goal for your first 90 days.
2. Retrospect yourself if that goal is in the right path...
3. Bring up these retrospection with your manager to check if you're on the right path...

### Take ownership of the little things
1. Find if you can contribute to the team in a small way like,
	1. Documentation
	2. Automation
	3. Optimisation
	4. Fixing a tech debt

### Stay Open to Feedback
1. Get Feed back from the team and manager
2. It's a good way to know about the team and your place in it

### Use the Product as a Customer
1. Try out the product and get the feel of what you'll be working with
2. Try to understand what the user will go through using your product
3. Bring forth suggestions, questions, and improvements to the team
