Documenting work helps grow as a developer.
Keep it as simple as possible.

##### Template
1. `Date` - Specific Date or Week / Month
2. `Tech Stack` - All about the tech stack followed in the project 
3. `Client Detail` - Company Name, Point of Contact, Website
4. `Work Highlight` - A list of 3 - 4 contributions, which are personal, with clear action and result.
